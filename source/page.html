Samples, interpretation and provenance

The expanded archive has 277 records. The original caption analysis uses 235 original English captions with at least 15 whitespace-separated tokens. The ranking and individual response distribution use 246 original activities with both counters, excluding reposts. Topic and approach labels are inherited from the original audit. The 21 additions are present in the archive but excluded from the original analytical comparisons.

Baseline counters are preserved from the supplied package, whose counters were collected on 1 October 2026 UTC. Browser checks were made on 2 October 2026. Thirty-seven counter differences are retained separately. Missing visible counters are unknown, not zero. Public response is cumulative and not normalised by impressions or matched post age. Twenty additions lack a resolved activity ID; all 21 lack an independently resolved exact UTC time. Full-account completeness is unverified.

The 28 selected figures are rendered here using D3 7.9.0, with their original report snapshots available below. The Word images are raster exports of those original SVGs. The interactive explorers recompute descriptive views from selected subsets. The figure manifest records the SVG and PNG hashes and the original figure mapping. Main images preserve aspect ratio, including in mosaics. Preview compression does not change the locally saved originals. One record has no standalone visual.

The palette algorithm groups dominant saturated hues and neutral images. It is exploratory pixel analysis, not a semantic classification or a test of effectiveness. The visual-type inventory describes the saved asset, which can be a document cover, video cover or single still.
