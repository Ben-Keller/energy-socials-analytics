Reactions and comments over time

Reactions and comments follow different paths. In the original caption sample, the annual median was 86 reactions and 2 comments in 2024, 69 and 4 in 2025, and 54 and 3 in January to September 2026. Recent posts have had less time to accumulate public response, so this pattern cannot establish a decline in reach.

Quarterly medians. Periods with fewer than three captions are suppressed. Counts are cumulative values preserved from the supplied package.

Writing and response / 04

Longer captions and more AI mentions

Median caption length rose from 86 words in 2024 to 296 in 2026, while explicit AI mentions rose from 24 percent to 63 percent. This is a shift in the observed editorial mix. It supports making each post’s particular decision easier to identify, rather than assuming that length itself is the cause of response.

Sample and interpretation

2026 covers January to September. AI means the supplied explicit AI or artificial intelligence indicator, including hashtags. Word counts include links and hashtags.

Writing and response / 05

Monthly length and public response

Across monthly medians, the supplied rank correlation is −0.27 for length and reactions and 0.42 for length and comments. These associations move in different directions. Year, topic, audience exposure and post age change at the same time, so shortening a caption cannot be expected to produce a specific response lift.

Sample and interpretation

Each bubble is a month. Bubble area represents the number of eligible captions. These are monthly comparisons, not estimates of individual post effects.

A small number of discussions therefore contribute a substantial part of the observed total. This supports studying exceptional posts alongside the median, while keeping their individual contexts visible.

Writing and response / 16

Question marks and yearly response

A question mark alone is not associated with a higher yearly median comment count in this record. In 2025 and 2026 the medians are the same for captions with and without questions; in 2024, captions with questions have a lower median. The editorial test should concern the substance of a question, not its mere presence.

Sample and interpretation

Question presence means at least one question mark anywhere in the caption. Counts and medians are descriptive and do not measure question quality.

Additional comparisons / 23

Topic distributions beyond the median

The middle half of each topic’s reaction counts gives context that a single median hides. Wide and overlapping intervals mean that many posts in different topics receive similar response. A strong outlier can coexist with an ordinary median, so topic decisions should combine the distribution with close reading of individual examples.

Sample and interpretation

Boxes span the 25th to 75th percentile. A dark dot marks the median; pale points show every caption. Horizontal position is reactions. Vertical jitter is deterministic and has no meaning.

03 / Sources & files

What the record can tell us

The expanded archive contains 277 recovered records. Original caption comparisons use 235 eligible English captions; response rankings use 246 original activities with both counters, excluding reposts. The atlas shows the expanded record and uses known counters for its response views.

Baseline counters were collected on 1 October 2026 UTC and browser checks made on 2 October. They are cumulative public counts, not reach or engagement rates. Missing counters are unknown. The 2023 sample is incomplete; recovered counts do not establish annual posting totals. Full-account completeness remains unverified. Twenty additions lack resolved activity IDs and all 21 additions lack independently resolved exact UTC times.

Colour families describe image pixels, not semantic meaning. The saved visual may be a document or video cover. One post has no standalone visual. Images retain their original aspect ratios.
